Hi! I am a postdoctoral researcher in the lab of
[Prof. Christopher S. Chen](https://www.bu.edu/eng/profile/christopher-chen-m-d-ph-d/) at
Boston University and the Wyss Institute at Harvard University,
working at the intersection of advanced microfabrication and tissue engineering.

I completed my PhD at the [Mechanical Engineering department](https://www.bu.edu/eng/academics/departments-and-divisions/mechanical-engineering/) and [Photonics Center](https://www.bu.edu/photonics/) of Boston University where I worked with [Prof. Kamil Ekinci](https://www.bu.edu/eng/profile/kamil-ekinci-ph-d/) and [Prof. Alice White](https://www.bu.edu/photonics/profile/alice-white/). 
Previously, I received my B.Sc. and M.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering at [Bilkent University](https://w3.bilkent.edu.tr/bilkent/). In my graduate studies, I also worked in [National Nanotechnology Research Center](https://unam.bilkent.edu.tr/en/) advised by [Prof. Selim Hanay](https://me.bilkent.edu.tr/4-people/536-selim-hanay/).

**Research Interests:** MEMS/NEMS, organ-on-chip, cardiac tissue engineering, 2-photon direct laser writing, nanomechanical sensors, microfluidics, scalable tissue platforms
