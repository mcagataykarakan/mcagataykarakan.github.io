During my PhD in Boston University, I worked as a graduate student teacher in [ME310 - Instrumentation](https://www.bu.edu/academics/eng/courses/eng-me-310/) course for two semesters. Weekly led lab sessions, guided students through practical experiments involving mechanical and electrical transducers (for flow, pressure, temperature, velocity, strain, and force) and analog and signal conditioning circuits. Ensured all students adhere to safety considerations and professional protocols within the active laboratory workspace. Graded formal laboratory reports based on professional standards and provided feedback on the quality of documentation, data interpretation, and formal statistical uncertainty analysis.    

During my M.Sc. in Bilkent University, I worked as a teaching assistant in [ME384 - Mechatronic Systems](https://catalog.bilkent.edu.tr/course/c16384.html), [ME341 - Dynamics and Control I](https://catalog.bilkent.edu.tr/course/c16341.html), and [ME 481 Mechanical Engineering Design I](https://catalog.bilkent.edu.tr/course/c16481.html) courses.

I also acted as a mentor in research experience programs for undergraduates ([REU](https://www.bu.edu/photonics-programs/reu/programs/)), teachers ([RET](https://www.bu.edu/photonics-programs/reu/programs/)) and high school students ([RISE](https://www.bu.edu/summer/high-school-programs/rise-internship-practicum/)). Some of my projects I developed for these programs can be found through this [link](https://www.bu.edu/photonics-programs/profile/mustafa-cagatay-karakan/).
