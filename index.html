M. Çağatay Karakan

Postdoctoral Researcher · Chen Lab · Boston University & Wyss Institute at Harvard University

📍 Boston, MA

Hi! I am a postdoctoral researcher in the lab of Prof. Christopher S. Chen at Boston University and the Wyss Institute at Harvard University, working at the intersection of advanced microfabrication and tissue engineering.

I completed my PhD at the Mechanical Engineering department and Photonics Center of Boston University where I worked with Prof. Kamil Ekinci and Prof. Alice White. Previously, I received my B.Sc. and M.S. degrees in Mechanical Engineering at Bilkent University. In my graduate studies, I also worked in National Nanotechnology Research Center advised by Prof. Selim Hanay.

Research Interests: MEMS/NEMS, organ-on-chip, cardiac tissue engineering, 2-photon direct laser writing, nanomechanical sensors, microfluidics, scalable tissue platforms
